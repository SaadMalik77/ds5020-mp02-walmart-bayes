To: Maria Lopez, Regional Operations Manager
From: Retail Analytics Team
Subject: Extra staff: which weeks?

1. A store has a strong week (sales at least 10% above its own average) in about 12.6% of store-weeks, or about 1 week in 8.
2. Thanksgiving is the holiday that matters most (about 82% of Thanksgiving weeks are strong), and November–December is the season that matters most (41% of those weeks are strong, about 3.3 times the overall rate).
3. The holiday rule is right only 27% of the time when it says "busy" and misses 85% of strong weeks, because most strong weeks are not holiday weeks; the Nov–Dec rule is better, at 41% precision and 41% recall, but still misses 59%.
4. Our Naive Bayes model does not beat the holiday rule: on 2012 weeks it never saw, it flagged no week at the usual 50% cut-off, and at a 10% cut-off it was right 15% of the time and found 7.5% of strong weeks, against 26% and 9% for the holiday rule.
5. The main risk is that 2012 has no Thanksgiving or Nov–Dec weeks, so the test is harder than a full year and a full-year result could differ, and with only 45 stores and data ending in October 2012 these numbers should not be treated as a forecast.
